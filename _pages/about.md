I am a PhD candidate at Utrecht University under supervision of Prof. dr. Chris Van Den Broeck, and also affiliated with Nikhef (Dutch National Institute for Subatomic Physics). My research focuses on Bayesian data analysis tools for gravitational waves, multi-messenger astrophysics and constraints on the nuclear equation of states of neutron stars, applied to future detectors like the Einstein Telescope.
